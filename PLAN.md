# The Wax Desk — Business Plan

## The research (who has money, who pays)

The hobby is not small. Collectr mid-year 2026: 28M tracked sales in H1, on pace for 50M+, average collection $2,475, online converts spend $1,000+ in month one, sealed +37% YTD. 3,256 US card stores. Fanatics Live on a $928M 2026 pace; top shop ~$15M/month.

People with money exist. They do **not** pay for what we were originally selling (media to free YouTube watchers).

| Who | Has money? | Pays us? |
|---|---|---|
| Person about to buy a $300–$3,000 SKU tonight | Yes | Pays for the **card**. We take a disclosed 3.5–5% cut. |
| LCS / flipper / TCGplayer seller | Yes. One wrong case is rent. | **$19/week** not to load the trap. |
| YouTube/X watcher | Volume, thin wallet | No. Gaming integrations start $50–$500. Ignore as buyer. |
| Serious collector already on Card Ladder | Yes | Already pays $15–$50/mo for **data**. We will not beat PSA's parent on 100M sales. |

Existence proof: Sports Card Investor (~$3.7M, 26 people) — after a data product **and** a quarter-million-sub channel. A rate card with no reach is a costume.

## Why not pivot

- **Home-services quoting:** contractors pay $30–$400/mo. Jobber, Housecall Pro, QuoteIQ, ServiceTitan own it. We would ship a toy.
- **Generic AI SaaS:** 7,600+ sites, 12.5% even have payments, median indie SaaS ~$145 MRR.
- **Finance Substack:** highest willingness-to-pay, zero of our credibility.

Stay in cards. Change the customer.
